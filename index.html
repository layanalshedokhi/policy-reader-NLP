About Policy Reader

Policy Reader uses RoBERTa-base, fine-tuned on UNFAIR-ToS: sentences from 50 real Terms of Service, labeled by legal experts with eight types of potentially unfair clauses.

How a sentence is flagged

The model gives each sentence a probability for each clause type. A type is flagged when its probability passes a cutoff tuned for that type on validation data. After error analysis, the cutoff for “Contract by using” was lowered to 0.5 in this app; the reported scores use the original cutoffs.

How risk levels are set

The model detects clause types, not risk levels. Each type has a fixed level by its likely effect on you. The overall level uses weighted risk points per 1,000 words, so long policies are not penalized for length alone.

High: liability, termination, arbitration Medium: change, removal, jurisdiction Low: choice of law, contract by using

Limits

  • It recognizes typical wording, not legal reasoning, so a clause with a fair justification can still be flagged.
  • It was trained on English consumer Terms of Service, so other kinds of documents may give less reliable results.
  • It finds about 3 in 4 unfair clauses, so some may be missed.
  • This is not legal advice.
